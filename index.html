Regulatory Policy & HHP Risk Matrix

Under PTAC 2026, 1,292 registered formulations (21.05%) meet the criteria for Highly Hazardous Pesticides (HHPs). These are categorized into regulatory risk tiers to guide phased market withdrawal and replacement.

⚠ Mandatory Extension Notice: All RED Tier products (607 formulations) are subject to active regulatory review. Extension workers are strictly prohibited from recommending RED Tier active ingredients where viable Standard or Green Tier alternatives exist, particularly in leafy export vegetables and peri-urban water catchment zones.

Acute Field Poisoning Response

Golden Rule: Immediately remove patient from contaminated zone, strip contaminated clothing, wash skin with clean water and soap. Never induce vomiting if swallowed pesticide contains hydrocarbon solvents (EC formulations) or if patient is unconscious.

Bio-Pesticides & Special Categories

Bio-Pesticides (43 products)

Comprising microbial fungicides (Trichoderma, Pseudomonas fluorescens), botanical fungicides (physcion), botanical insecticides (azadirachtin), viral insecticides (Spodoptera NPV), and semiochemical lures (methyl eugenol, cuelure, BSFB pheromone, fall armyworm pheromone). The majority do not yet carry an IRAC/FRAC/HRAC MoA code.

Pheromone Lures & Mass Trapping (21 products)

Methyl eugenol lures target Bactrocera dorsalis on mango and guava; cuelure lures target Bactrocera cucurbitae on bitter gourd; BSFB pheromone lures target brinjal shoot and fruit borer; fall armyworm pheromone lures for maize. Standard rate: 40–100 lures per hectare.

Stored-Grain Fumigation (102 products)

Dominated by aluminium phosphide fumigation (Phostoxin, Celphos, Quick-Fume). Releases phosphine gas (IRAC 24A) inhibiting mitochondrial respiration. Standard dose: 3–4 tablets per metric tonne, sealed storage, 5–7 day exposure.

Rodenticides (18 products)

Zinc phosphide (acute, IRAC 24A, 2% bait for burrow baiting) and Bromadiolone (anticoagulant, bait block). Rotate between acute and anticoagulant to avoid resistance. Both toxic to non-target mammals — secure bait stations required.
