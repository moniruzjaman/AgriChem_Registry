Acute Field Poisoning Response

Golden Rule: Immediately remove patient from contaminated zone, strip contaminated clothing, wash skin with clean water and soap. Never induce vomiting if swallowed pesticide contains hydrocarbon solvents (EC formulations) or if patient is unconscious.
