About this registry

One de-duplicated catalogue of registered pesticide products, reconciled across the DAE PTAC-approved list. MoA codes mapped by active ingredient (IRAC/FRAC/HRAC). Unmapped products are left visibly unmapped rather than guessed.

⚠ Field-reference extract, not a regulatory status check. Always confirm current approval, dose and pre-harvest interval against the official DAE register before agricultural use.
